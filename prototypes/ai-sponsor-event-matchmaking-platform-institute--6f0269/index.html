Competitive prototype

AI Sponsor-Event Matchmaking Platform

Automated marketplace that matches B2B AI vendors with curated executive events (summits, dinners, workshops) based on target-account fit, budget, and calendar, replacing manual outreach with data-driven inventory allocation.

Direct access to curated senior AI, data, and technology executives from Fortune 500 enterprises (banks, healthcare, retail, energy) through high-touch speaking, sponsorship, and private dinner formats.

Core features

  • Sponsor profile ingestion (ICP, budget, preferred conferences, speaking topics)
  • Real-time event inventory sync (panel slots, dinner caps, workshop hosts) from organizer feeds
  • Match scoring engine ranking events by attendee overlap with sponsor target accounts
  • Automated proposal generator producing customized sponsorship packages with pricing tiers

Differentiators

  • Two-sided network: organizers publish inventory once; sponsors discover and book without email ping-pong
  • Account-level attendee intelligence (anonymized) to prove ROI before commit
  • Post-event attribution dashboard linking leads to specific sessions or dinners

MVP plan

  1. Ingest public summit schedules and known sponsor lists (Lenovo, NVIDIA, Intel, Dell) into a searchable catalog
  2. Build sponsor onboarding form capturing ICP, budget range, and conference calendar
  3. Prototype match algorithm using keyword overlap between sponsor topics and panel titles
  4. Generate one-page PDF proposal per match for pilot with 3-5 sponsors

Risks

  • Organizers may withhold granular attendee data, limiting match quality
  • Sponsors accustomed to relationship-based sales may resist self-serve booking
  • Seasonal event calendar creates lumpy revenue and uneven platform utilization
