Competitive Installed-Base Mapping Platform

Displacement-focused sales intelligence for manufacturing teams that reveals competitor contract details and refresh timing at the account level.

Enables displacement selling by revealing exactly where competitors are entrenched, with contract-level detail and refresh timing, so reps can prioritize high-probability accounts.

Core features

  • Account-level competitor installed-base mapping with verified contacts
  • Contract detail tracking including renewal dates and terms
  • Priority scoring to rank displacement opportunities

Differentiators

  • Manufacturing-specific focus on entrenched competitor displacement
  • Contract-level granularity with refresh date intelligence
  • Priority scoring tailored for displacement selling motions

MVP plan

  1. Define target manufacturing verticals and competitor set
  2. Build data pipeline for contract and refresh date aggregation
  3. Develop priority scoring algorithm and sales UI

Risks

  • Data accuracy and freshness for contract-level details
  • Legal and compliance around competitive intelligence gathering
  • Adoption friction if not integrated into existing CRM workflows
